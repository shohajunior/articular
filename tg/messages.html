🚀ArticularUZ

We believe that having a great idea is only the beginning.
Whether it is a scientific discovery, a space mission, or a solution to a real-world problem — an idea creates impact when you can understand it, explain it, defend it, and make others see why it matters.
That is what ArticularUZ is about.
A youth-led initiative where students explore science and space, develop ideas, think critically, work in teams, and learn to communicate what they know clearly and confidently.
Our main areas:
🛰 Science & Space — exploring scientific ideas and the universe around us
🧠 Critical Thinking — questioning assumptions and looking at problems from different perspectives
🎤 Public Speaking — expressing ideas clearly, confidently, and effectively
🤝 Teamwork — collaborating with others to develop and refine ideas
💡 Idea Communication — turning complex thoughts into clear, understandable explanations
📌 Who is ArticularUZ for?
• Young people interested in science and new ideas
• Students who want to become better communicators
• Those looking to develop their critical thinking and public speaking skills
• Young people ready to challenge themselves, collaborate, and grow
But ArticularUZ is more than a single event.
This is the beginning of a bigger vision: to build a nationwide community of young thinkers and communicators across Uzbekistan — people who can understand complex ideas, communicate them effectively, and collaborate to develop them further.

Learn. Think. Articulate. 🚀

ArticularUZ — Event Recap🚀
Our second event is officially in the books — and once again, our participants outdid themselves, surprising us even more than last time with their sharpness and ingenuity.

Getting to Know Uzcosmos🔭
The event opened with a welcome speech from Ikrom Alimukhamedov, Specialist in the Department for the Implementation of New Technologies and Projects — our mentor from the Uzcosmos agency and a member of our judging panel — took the stage to go deeper: walking participants through the groundbreaking opportunities the agency offers, the latest developments in the space sector, and exclusive data available only to people in the room.🛰

Preparation Time: Learn & Think 🧠
Then the timer started ticking. Each team had just 20 minutes to grasp a space-related topic on deep space exploration — and then explain it to the entire auditorium. The room came alive with energy: teams discussing, collaborating, and digging into the material together.💬

Time to Articulate🎙
One by one, every team took the stage — and each team had up to 6 minutes to present. Despite having only 20 minutes to prepare, every team managed to present with striking clarity.🔝


Recognizing Excellence 🏆
Top three best-performing teams were awarded official Certificates of Appreciation by the Uzcosmos agency. 🎖️
A huge thank-you to everyone who made this possible. Stay tuned — it is only the beginning!

Learn. Think. Articulate.🚀
